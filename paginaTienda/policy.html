Privacy policy

A simple notice about the responsible use of information.

Manga Ancha is an academic demo project. We are committed to handling information transparently and following privacy principles, such as using only the data that is necessary and explaining what it is used for.

What information is used?

The demo features may use the data entered in the forms, as well as the information needed to display the catalogue or remember the products in the basket in the browser.

What is it used for?

The information is used to show the features of the sample store, such as the account, the catalogue and the basket. Sensitive information should not be entered in this demo.

Privacy and rights

Data protection regulations recognize rights such as accessing, correcting or requesting the deletion of personal data. In a real service, this page should identify the data controller, explain the applicable legal bases and retention periods, and provide a contact channel to exercise those rights.

This text is for information only and is part of an academic assignment; on its own it does not certify the legal compliance of a real store. Before using the project as a commercial service, the specific information about its data controller and the actual processing of data would need to be completed and reviewed.
